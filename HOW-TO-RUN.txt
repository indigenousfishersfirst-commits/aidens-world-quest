Aiden's World Quest - how to run it

ON A PC (works with no internet)
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.
3. Press the full-screen button at the top, or press F11 (Esc leaves full screen).

ON AN ANDROID TABLET
Best: open the game's web link in Chrome, open the Chrome menu (three dots),
choose "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". It then opens full screen like an app
and keeps working offline after the first visit.

SAVING
Use "Save & continue" in the game to copy an adventure code. Paste it back next time.
