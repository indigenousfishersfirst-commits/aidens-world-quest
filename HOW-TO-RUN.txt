Aiden's World Quest - how to run it

ON A PC (works with no internet)
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome or Edge is best).
3. Tap "Start with music and voice". Press the full-screen button at the top, or press F11 (Esc leaves full screen).
4. Turn the volume up. Music, narration and sound effects are all included in the folder.

ON AN ANDROID TABLET
Best: open the game's web link in Chrome, open the Chrome menu (three dots),
choose "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". It then opens full screen like an app
and keeps working offline after the first visit (all audio is saved too).
Tablet sound: check the media volume is up and the tablet is not on silent.

WHAT IS NEW
- Every story is read aloud with the words highlighted. Questions and choices are read too.
- Original adventure music, with a faster battle tune. Music gets quieter while the voice speaks.
- Adventure club: Trail Dash (hop over obstacles), Taste Match (food and country memory game),
  local friends to chat with in each country, and Friendship Arena Pokemon battles.
- Win a battle to earn a badge and a bonus Pokemon. 12 Pokemon friends to collect.
- XP and explorer levels. No timers on questions and no key can ever be lost.

SAVING
Use "Save & continue" in the game to copy an adventure code (AWQ2). Paste it back next time.
Older AWQ1 codes still work.
